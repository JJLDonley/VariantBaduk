# Privacy Policy

Variant Go Server does not track people for analytics, advertising, profiling, or resale.

## What we store

We store only what is needed to make the game work:

- a random session/player id so the server can remember which games and seats are yours;
- your public display name, if you choose one;
- game records, moves, seats, timestamps, settings, and results;
- necessary session/auth cookies.

## What we do not store or use

- No analytics trackers.
- No ad trackers.
- No behavioral profiling.
- No sale of personal data.
- No email addresses or payment data are required by the app.

## Server logs

The hosting environment may keep normal operational logs such as request times, paths, and IP
addresses for security, debugging, and uptime. These are not used for advertising or profiling.

## Cookies

Cookies are used only to keep a session id/auth token so your browser can rejoin games and seats
correctly.

## Retention

Game and player records remain in the database until deleted by an administrator or database cleanup
process. You can change your public display name at any time.
